02 About

Engineer first. Bridge between R&D and manufacturing.

I started on the shop floor and at the CAD bench, and I still design from there.

My career began designing powertrain tooling, fixtures and gearbox test systems at Mahindra Farm Machinery, where hardware had to survive production, not just review. Since then I've managed BOM and ECO control and NPI builds on automation-system assemblies, and I now lead consumer electronics programs at Hyundai Technology. There I review prototypes and mechanical and industrial design, and connect our R&D team with ODM partners in India and China.

Across all of it, I use real evidence like teardowns, CAD overlays, FEA and 3D-printed fit checks to close design, fit and quality issues before tooling is cut.

Design

SolidWorks (CSWP), NX, Creo, CATIA. Fixtures, weldments, sheet metal, GD&T.

Validate

ANSYS FEA, Fluent and Icepak, prototype builds, root-cause analysis.

Deliver

DFM, BOM and ECO/ECN, PLM/ERP, supplier and ODM management across 3 countries.
